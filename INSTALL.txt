KRAKEN V15 COURSE DROPDOWN HOVER FIX

Replace:
- kraken-nav.js

Then add the contents of:
- course-hover-fix.css

to the BOTTOM of kraken-global.css.

Or simply replace the dropdown CSS in kraken-global.css with this file.

WHAT CHANGES

Desktop:
- Hovering Courses opens the dropdown immediately.
- Moving the mouse down into the dropdown keeps it open.
- A short 180ms close delay prevents flicker.
- Hovering individual course links works normally.
- The arrow rotates when open.

Mobile:
- Tapping Courses opens/closes the menu.
- It does not rely on hover.

Keyboard:
- Tab/focus opens it.
- Escape closes it.

The list remains dynamic from Supabase, so newly published courses
automatically appear under their category.
